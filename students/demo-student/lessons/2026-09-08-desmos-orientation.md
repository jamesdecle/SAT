### Desmos move demo
Type `y1=2x-3` and `y2=7` on two lines, click the intersection point, read
off x. Then add a slider: type `y1=a*x-3`, click "add slider" on `a`, and
drag it to see the line rotate.

In the meantime, a content-free Desmos fluency drill:
1. Graph `y=x^2-4` and click the two x-intercepts.
2. Graph `y=x^2-4` and click the vertex.
3. Build a table with x1: 1,2,3,4,5 and y1: 3,5,7,9,11; type `y1 ~ mx1+b`
   and read off the slope and intercept.
4. Type `(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=9` and identify the center and radius by eye.
